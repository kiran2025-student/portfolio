About

I'm an Electronics and Communication student at Marwadi University. I like work where code meets a physical device: a motor that has to turn, a sensor that has to report, a drone that has to fly.

I'm the Vice President of the Aero Modelling Club, where I coordinate members working on drones and fixed-wing planes. I'm also an IEEE student member in the Robotics and Automation Society (RAS).
